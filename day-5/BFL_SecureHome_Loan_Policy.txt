BFL SECUREHOME LOAN — PRODUCT POLICY DOCUMENT (SYNTHETIC / TRAINING USE ONLY)
==============================================================================
Note: This is a fictional product created for training purposes only. It does
not represent an actual Bajaj Finserv product, rate, or policy.

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
   Product Name: BFL SecureHome Loan
   Category: Retail Home Loan
   Issuing Entity (fictional): BFL Housing Finance Group

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   - Minimum applicant age: 23 years at loan origination
   - Maximum applicant age at loan maturity: 65 years
   - Minimum net monthly income: INR 45,000 (salaried) / INR 60,000 (self-employed)
   - Minimum credit bureau score: 725
   - Employment continuity: 2 years for salaried, 3 years for self-employed

3. LOAN TERMS
   - Minimum loan tenure: 5 years
   - Maximum loan tenure: 25 years
   - Minimum loan amount: INR 10,00,000
   - Maximum loan amount: INR 5,00,00,000 (subject to property valuation and
     income multiple caps)
   - Loan-to-Value (LTV) ratio: up to 80% for loans below INR 30,00,000; up to
     75% for loans between INR 30,00,001 and INR 75,00,000; up to 70% above
     that threshold

4. INTEREST RATE STRUCTURE
   - Rate type: Floating, linked to BFL Reference Benchmark Rate (fictional)
   - Indicative rate range: 8.35% – 9.90% per annum, risk-based pricing
   - Rate reset frequency: Quarterly

5. FEES AND CHARGES
   - Processing fee: 0.50% of loan amount + applicable taxes (minimum INR
     5,000, maximum INR 15,000)
   - Prepayment charges: Nil for individual borrowers on floating-rate loans
   - Late payment charge: 2% per month on overdue EMI amount
   - Legal and technical valuation fee: At actuals, payable by borrower

6. PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE
   - Part-prepayment allowed after 6 EMIs have been serviced
   - Maximum part-prepayment per calendar year: 25% of outstanding principal
   - Full foreclosure permitted any time after 12 months, subject to Nil
     charges for individual floating-rate borrowers

7. DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
   - KYC documents (identity and address proof)
   - Income proof (salary slips / ITR as applicable)
   - Property title documents and No Objection Certificate
   - Bank statements for the last 6 months

8. SPECIAL CONDITIONS
   - Co-applicant income can be clubbed for eligibility computation
   - Step-up repayment option available for applicants under 35 years of age
   - Insurance bundling (fire and life) is optional, not mandatory

END OF DOCUMENT
