BFL FLEXIBIZ WORKING CAPITAL LOAN — PRODUCT POLICY DOCUMENT (SYNTHETIC / TRAINING USE ONLY)
==============================================================================
Note: This is a fictional product created for training purposes only. It does
not represent an actual Bajaj Finserv product, rate, or policy.

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
   Product Name: BFL FlexiBiz Working Capital Loan
   Category: Unsecured Business Loan
   Issuing Entity (fictional): BFL Lending & Investments Group

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   - Business vintage: minimum 3 years of continuous operation
   - Minimum annual turnover: INR 40,00,000
   - Applicant age: 25–60 years at loan origination
   - Minimum credit bureau score: 700
   - Business must be profit-making in at least 2 of the last 3 financial years

3. LOAN TERMS
   - Minimum loan tenure: 12 months
   - Maximum loan tenure: 48 months
   - Minimum loan amount: INR 5,00,000
   - Maximum loan amount: INR 75,00,000 (unsecured)
   - Loans above INR 75,00,000 require collateral and move to the Secured
     Business Loan product (not covered in this document)

4. INTEREST RATE STRUCTURE
   - Rate type: Fixed for entire tenure
   - Indicative rate range: 13.50% – 18.00% per annum, risk-based pricing
   - Rate depends on business vintage, turnover stability, and bureau score

5. FEES AND CHARGES
   - Processing fee: 2.00% of loan amount + applicable taxes
   - Prepayment charges: 4% of outstanding principal if foreclosed within 12
     months of disbursement; 2% thereafter
   - Late payment charge: 3% per month on overdue EMI amount
   - Documentation and stamp duty charges: At actuals, as per state
     regulations

6. PREPAYMENT AND FORECLOSURE
   - Part-prepayment not permitted in the first 6 months
   - Part-prepayment thereafter capped at 20% of outstanding principal per
     financial year
   - Full foreclosure permitted after 12 months, subject to applicable
     foreclosure charges described in Section 5

7. DOCUMENTATION REQUIRED
   - KYC documents for all partners / directors
   - Business registration and GST certificate
   - Last 3 years audited financial statements
   - Last 12 months current account bank statements
   - Existing loan repayment track record, if any

8. SPECIAL CONDITIONS
   - Top-up loan available after 12 months of clean repayment history,
     subject to fresh eligibility check
   - Seasonal businesses may request a customized repayment schedule
     (bullet or step-down EMI), subject to credit committee approval
   - This product does not offer a moratorium period

END OF DOCUMENT
